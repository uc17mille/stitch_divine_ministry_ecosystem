Key Concepts: Servant Leadership

In this lesson, we explore the paradox of power. True spiritual leadership is not found in the exercise of authority over others, but in the radical commitment to serve. We examine the biblical foundations of the "Heart of a Servant" and how that translates to modern organizational stewardship.
